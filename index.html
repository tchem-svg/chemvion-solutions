About Chemvion

Connecting Africa to the
Bioplastics Revolution

Chemvion Solutions is a specialised distributor of bio-based and compostable resins, bridging world-leading polymer producers with manufacturers and converters across Africa.

We carry stock in key logistics hubs, manage import documentation, and provide hands-on technical support — so our partners can focus on production, not procurement.

From flexible packaging in Lagos to rigid food-service ware in Nairobi, Chemvion delivers the grades, the volumes and the expertise that African industry needs to transition away from fossil-based plastics.

Bio-Based Resins for

01
PLA
Polylactic Acid

Derived from fermented plant sugars — corn, cassava, sugarcane. The most widely used biopolymer globally, available in multiple flow grades for injection moulding, film, fibre and thermoforming.

Rigid Packaging Food Contact Film & Sheet 3D Print
02
PHA
Polyhydroxyalkanoates

Microbially produced in-cell polyesters — truly marine and soil biodegradable. Premium-grade PHAs for applications where end-of-life performance is non-negotiable.

Marine-Safe Soil Biodeg. Medical Agriculture
03
PBAT / PBS
Compostable Blending Resins

Flexible, industrially compostable copolymers used as blend partners with PLA to improve toughness and elongation. Essential for soft packaging, bags and agricultural mulch film.

Our Commitment

Built for a
Circular Economy

Green-grade compostable bioplastic pellets — EN 13432 certified
C
Compostable

Our resins meet EN 13432 and ASTM D6400 standards for industrial compostability. Select grades achieve home-compost certification — returning nutrients to soil at every product's end of life.

R
Renewable

Every tonne of bio-based resin we distribute is derived from annually renewable feedstocks — corn starch, sugarcane, cassava and agricultural residues — decoupling plastics from fossil resources.

R
Recyclable

Working with brands and municipalities to establish closed-loop collection programmes. PLA can be chemically recycled back to lactic acid — re-entering production as virgin-quality resin.

Why Africa

Serving
the Continent

Africa produces over 17 million tonnes of plastic annually — yet bio-based and compostable alternatives account for less than 0.3% of that volume. The gap between global innovation and local availability is where Chemvion operates.

We have built the relationships, logistics infrastructure and regulatory knowledge needed to move speciality resins from source to factory floor across the continent — efficiently and at scale.
